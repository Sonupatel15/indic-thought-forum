# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The public audience is anyone curious about Indic Thought Forum and its work, with particular focus on young people. The audience is primarily Indian, including people living in India and people living outside India. Visitors want to understand the forum, learn about Bharateeya heritage and its activities, and find ways to participate.

## Product Purpose

Indic Thought Forum (ITF) creates awareness of Bharateeya Sabhyata and seeks to turn that awareness into meaningful action. It connects people with India's cultural and intellectual heritage through learning, dialogue, events, and community initiatives, and helps them find practical ways to contribute.

## Positioning

ITF is an Indic, culturally rooted, nationalistic forum that connects awareness with action. Its public website brings heritage, conversations, events, and Sankalpa-based commitments together as ways for people to engage and contribute.

## Operating Context

The website is a public information and participation channel for visitors in India and abroad. Visitors can learn about ITF, explore cultural and intellectual content, review event listings and schedules, browse updates and community media, and consider membership, volunteering, or other contribution pathways. Published event information includes dates, India Standard Time timings, venues, and event posters where available; current venue listings include Bengaluru.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The public site includes Home, About and Philosophy, Sankalpas, Updates, Calendar, Contact, Support, Privacy, Accessibility, and not-found pages. Updates brings together the event archive, gallery, podcasts, community posts, and social links.
- Visitors can explore a Sankalpa tracker and personal contribution pathways, browse confirmed event information, and use public contact or membership forms when a verified service is configured.
- The site is a static export built with Next.js App Router, React, and TypeScript. It has no database, admin dashboard, authentication, payment processing, or form backend.
- Form submission and online donations remain unavailable until verified public HTTPS services are configured. The optional public contact email is also unset until verified.
- Site configuration is supplied at build time. Public environment variables must not contain secrets; configured service URLs and email addresses are validated.
- Current event content and tracker records are published data, not evidence of an administrative or real-time management system.

## Brand Commitments

- The organization is named Indic Thought Forum (ITF).
- Preserve the organization's Indic and culturally rooted identity and its stated nationalistic character without inferring additional political positions.
- Preserve the terms “Bharateeya Sabhyata” and “Sankalpa” as part of the organization's vocabulary. A Sankalpa is described on the site as a conscious commitment.
- The existing site messaging centers awareness, respectful dialogue, learning, community participation, and purposeful action.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing public site copy describing ITF's purpose, principles, and participation pathways.
- Published event listings, schedules, and event posters.
- Community photographs, podcast information and thumbnails, and official social-channel links.
- A supplied Sankalpa tracker and contribution-pathway content.
- The project does not provide confirmed outcome metrics or third-party service details. Do not invent impact claims, endorsements, testimonials, or service availability.

## Product Principles

1. Connect awareness of cultural heritage to meaningful, practical action.
2. Make Bharateeya cultural and intellectual knowledge relevant to contemporary life.
3. Make room for curiosity, respectful dialogue, and learning.
4. Offer multiple ways to participate, including events, personal commitments, service, and sharing skills.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The existing website documents keyboard interactions, reduced-motion support, and automated WCAG A/AA checks across its public routes. These automated checks do not establish complete real-world conformance; manual testing with assistive technologies and physical devices remains necessary before public launch. No additional product-specific accessibility standard or user need has been confirmed.
